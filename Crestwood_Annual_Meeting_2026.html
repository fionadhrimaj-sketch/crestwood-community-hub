Annual Meeting of Shareholders

Tuesday, October 27, 2026

7:00 PM • In person • Downstairs basement area
Two Board of Directors seats are up for election.
Proxy deadline: All proxies must be received by 4:00 PM today, October 27, 2026, to be official. The official notice directs shareholders to email the signed Proxy/Ballot to MLuginsland@metromanagement.com.

Official Meeting Information

Purpose of the Meeting

1. To elect two (2) directors to the Board of Directors.
2. To take such other action as may properly come before the meeting.

Quorum

For a valid meeting to be held, shareholders representing 51% of the issued and outstanding shares must be present in person or by proxy.

Voting

The Proxy/Ballot contains the names of candidates eligible to serve on the Board. The notice states that no nominations will be taken at the meeting.

Questions

The notice asks shareholders to submit meeting questions to Litsa Douris at LDouris@metromanagement.com prior to the meeting.

Annual Meeting Agenda

  1. Call to order.
  2. Presentation of proofs of due calling of the meeting.
  3. Roll Call and presentation and examination of proxies.
  4. Reading of Minutes of previous meeting, unless waived.
  5. Reports of Officers and Committees.
  6. Appointment or election of inspectors of election, if requested.
  7. If the annual meeting or a special meeting called for that purpose, the election of directors.
  8. Unfinished business.
  9. New business.
  10. Adjournment.

Candidate Statements

Candidate-submitted statements are reproduced from the materials provided to shareholders. Formatting has been standardized for readability; handwritten notes are not included.

Geraldine Canal

Current Board member • Crestwood resident for over 43 years • Mental Health Counselor

As a Board Member for the past few years, I have realized how important it is to have a vested interest in our building. I have lived in the Crestwood for over 43 years. I have a true love for our building and community. I feel I have a good relationship with our tenants and shareholders.

As a professional Mental Health Counselor, I feel I have the ability to focus on the needs of our Crestwood community, especially our financial and emotional challenges. They have been plentiful these past years.

Being on the Board is a serious responsibility, one that I have not taken lightly. I believe that I am honest and can be open to opinions and expectations of our shareholders and tenants. Going forward my only hope, that the welfare of our building continues to prosper.

Nikolay Georgiev

10-year resident • 3 years Board service • Education and financial-analysis background
Education / Certification: Masters in Science in Education, Hunter College, NY • Certified Financial Analyst, CFA Level II, New York City, NY

Hello dear neighbors,

I lived in our cooperative for 10 years, including the past three years serving on the Board. I have had the opportunity to develop an understanding of our community, its strengths, and the challenges we face.

The last three years have been particularly challenging, with significant increases in operating costs, maintenance expenses, and the overall cost of maintaining our properties. During my time on the Board, I have advocated for finding ways to control and reduce maintenance and operating costs while continuing to maintain the quality and value of our homes.

If elected to continue serving on the Board, I will remain committed to identifying practical solutions that can help reduce unnecessary expenses and improve the financial health of our cooperative. I also believe we should actively explore new ideas, projects, and opportunities that can generate additional value and revenue for the cooperative. By finding responsible ways to bring in additional income and improve our properties, we can work toward benefiting all shareholders while strengthening the long-term value of our homes.

My experience in hospitality management has also provided me with valuable knowledge in budgeting, financial planning, operations, and facility management. I believe these skills, combined with my three years of Board experience and ten years as a resident, allow me to contribute meaningfully to responsible and thoughtful decision-making.

I also encourage shareholders to consider running for a Board position and becoming actively involved in the future of our cooperative. Bringing new ideas, knowledge, experience, and energy to the Board can strengthen our community and help us find creative solutions to the challenges we face. I believe that an engaged and diverse group of shareholders working together is essential to building a stronger cooperative.

I look forward to continuing to work collaboratively with the Board and our residents to address our challenges, explore new opportunities, manage our resources responsibly, and help make our cooperative a stronger and more valuable community for everyone.

Thank you for considering my candidacy. I would be honored to continue serving on the Board and contributing to the continued success and prosperity of our cooperative.
