Before work begins: The Board or managing agent determines current approval and insurance requirements. This shareholder-prepared guide does not authorize a contractor or replace management review.

Steps for residents and contractors

Step 1

Request current requirements

Contact management to confirm renovation approval, scheduling, access, and any applicable deposit or insurance requirements.

Step 2

Provide COI details

Give your contractor the COI instructions and sample, including the correct certificate holder and any required additional-insured wording.

Step 3

Obtain the certificate

Ask the contractor's insurance broker to issue a certificate meeting management's current stated requirements. Don't rely on an older sample for coverage limits.

Step 4

Submit for approval

Have management review the COI and approve the work before the contractor begins.
