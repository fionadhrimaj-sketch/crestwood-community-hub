Governance Quick Reference

A plain-language summary of several rules found in the Crestwood By-Laws.

Annual Shareholder MeetingThe annual meeting is for electing directors and other proper business. The By-Laws state it is to be held before May 1 at a time and place designated by the Board.
Meeting NoticeWritten notice of the annual meeting is to be delivered or mailed to eligible shareholders not less than 10 days and not more than 40 days before the meeting.
Special Shareholder MeetingA special meeting may be called by the president, secretary, or a majority of the Board. The secretary must also call one when shareholders owning at least 25% of the outstanding shares request it in writing.
Governing DocumentsThe By-Laws and Proprietary Lease should be read together when reviewing shareholder rights, Board authority, occupancy, elections, and other corporate matters.
