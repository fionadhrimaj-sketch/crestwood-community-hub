About the 1989 Schedule A and Historical Data
Historical information: This page reproduces selected columns from the 1989 Schedule A: apartment number, status, rooms, baths, and number of shares. The figures reflect the historical record and should not be treated as confirmation of current occupancy, regulatory status, apartment configuration, or current legal share allocation without checking the current proprietary lease, stock certificate, offering plan, or management records.
1989 status codes: S = Stabilized, C = Controlled, V = Vacant. These describe the conversion-era status shown in the source document and are not current resident or ownership statuses.
Historical source discrepancy: Crestwood has 72 apartments, while the photographed 1989 Schedule A contains 71 apartment rows and does not list Apartment 3H. No share allocation, room count, bath count, or 1989 status has been inferred for that unit.
