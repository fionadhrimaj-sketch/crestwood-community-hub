Gas Safety

If You Suspect a Gas Leak

  • Open nearby doors and windows and leave the building immediately.
  • Do not operate electrical switches, smoke, light matches, or use a phone inside the building.
  • From a safe distance, call 911.
  • Then call Con Edison at 1-800-752-6633.
