Safety & Building Information

Important fire-safety information and emergency guidance for Crestwood residents.

Fire Safety Notice

Crestwood is identified in this notice as a non-combustible (fireproof) building. The notice explains what residents should do if a fire is inside their apartment and what to do if a fire is elsewhere in the building.

Open Fire Safety Notice →

Key Fire Safety Reminders

If the fire is in your apartment: leave the apartment, take your keys, close but do not lock the apartment door, use the nearest stairwell, do not use the elevator, and call 911 once you are in a safe location.

If the fire is not in your apartment: generally stay inside and listen for firefighter instructions unless conditions become dangerous. If you must leave, first check your door and doorknob for heat and the hallway for smoke or fire.

Emergency: In the event of fire or another life-safety emergency, call 911 and follow instructions from the Fire Department. This Community Hub summary does not replace the official Fire Safety Notice.
